# Business

Turning what you build into something that earns. This area is for the builder who wants the work to compound into leverage, income, or a company — without the agency-flex noise.

Kynd's stance: own the asset. The work you do for someone else could be yours, with more return. Build in public; the proof is the pitch.

## What belongs here

- How to price, package, and sell agent-built services or products.
- Business models that fit a one-person builder (the "one-person business" shape).
- Case studies of Kynd members turning a build into income.
- The line between "cool demo" and "something someone pays for."
