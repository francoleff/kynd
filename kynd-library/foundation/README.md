# Foundation

Before you build, you need ground. This area answers the questions every beginner actually has:

- What is an AI agent, in plain words?
- What can it do, and what can't it?
- What are the words people keep using, and what do they mean?

If you skip this, the rest of the Library feels like a foreign language. Spend twenty minutes here and everything downstream gets easier.

## What belongs here

- Plain-language explanations of agents, models, tools, memory, context.
- The Kynd glossary (our shared vocabulary).
- The "should I even use an agent for this?" decision basics — the full version is [Should you use an agent for this?](../playbooks/should-you-use-an-agent.md) in Playbooks.
