# KYND : INTRO POST PACK (Post Zero)

**The angle : authority through honesty, not credentials.**
The hook is the gap: every AI community is courses or flexing, nobody helps beginners actually build. Lead with that.

**Voice rules (non-negotiable) : this is YOUR voice, learned from your real LinkedIn posts:**
- Open with an observation: "I've noticed a pattern."
- Ask mid-post questions: "How?" "Why?" "But why?" "The truth?"
- Short lines. Whitespace is your signature. Let thoughts breathe.
- Numbered lists. Capitalize one word for emphasis ("you WILL grow").
- Plain facts, no guru energy, no theater. "Simple, but mine."
- Never "not this, but that." No "Not because X. Because Y." No "You don't need X. You need Y." State the positive directly.
- Never "I run my agency with an agent." Say "I build with AI agents." The agency stays out of the copy.
- Never ADHD, depression, or mental-health confession.
- One CTA per post. End with a warm invite: "Let me know your thoughts" energy.
- If you wouldn't say it out loud, cut it.
- **Space every punctuation mark:** the mark gets its own space . "word ," "word ." "word ?" "word !" . Exactly like Franco types his prompts. Never attach punctuation to the word.

**One pillar -> four platforms.**
Write the essay once (Substack), then chop it. Same machine as week 1.

```
SUBSTACK essay (the deep version)
   +-- X thread (the reach engine)
   +-- LinkedIn post (the authority cut)
   +-- Reddit story (no links, value first)
```

**Structure:**
- Hook: "Everyone is waiting for permission."
- The enemy: "You need credentials first" . Dismantled
- The advantage: the Problem-First Method, Kynd, named frameworks
- 3 steps + one CTA

**One CTA per platform:**
| Platform | CTA |
|---|---|
| Substack | Reply / join the free Discord |
| X | Comment -> I send the invite |
| LinkedIn | Comment -> I send the invite |
| Reddit | None. Answer questions, build trust |

**Timing:** publish as **Post Zero** this weekend (Sat/Sun), then run week 1 as scheduled (Aug 10-16). Reddit goes a day after the others so it doesn't read as a spam wave.
